# Hi, I'm Aidan

I work in cyber security and technology risk in Perth, Western Australia.

My background sits in two places that do not often overlap. I spent several years assessing cyber security risk as an auditor. I now spend my time building and delivering the controls that mitigate cyber security risk. I have had the experience of being able to investigate issues and write findings, and now I create and deploy controls to address cyber security risk.

## What I do now

I am a Senior Cyber Security Specialist in the Cyber Security Unit at the Department of the Premier and Cabinet, Office of Digital Government. My job is to help WA Government agencies meet the WA Cyber Security Policy.

## Where I started

Before this I spent nearly four years as an information systems auditor at the Office of the Auditor General for Western Australia. I assessed technology risk across government and education entities. The work covered vendor and third party risk, application and cloud security, and general computer controls.

Before that I was a system administrator at the University of Notre Dame and Presbyterian Ladies' College.

## Essential Eight

I was named on the audit team for [Report 9: 2023-24, *Implementation of the Essential Eight Cyber Security Controls*](https://audit.wa.gov.au/reports-and-publications/reports/implementation-of-the-essential-eight-cyber-security-controls/), tabled in the Western Australian Parliament in December 2023. The audit found that seven of the ten entities had rated their own maturity higher than the evidence supported. The Office of Digital Government answered it with a four point program to improve how agencies self assess.

That report is the clearest example I have of research turning into something other people used.

## WACC

I maintain [WA Control Crosswalk](https://github.com/eltorago/WACC). It is an open source tool that maps controls across eighteen cyber security frameworks, including the WA Cyber Security Policy, the ASD Information Security Manual, ISO 27001 and NIST.

I chose which frameworks to include and defined the testing method behind each control. I then built the tool using AI coding tools under my own direction, refining it through repeated review rather than writing the code by hand.
